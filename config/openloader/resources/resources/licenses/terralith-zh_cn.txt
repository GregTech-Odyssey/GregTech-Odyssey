1.20.x v2.5.4 and their 14 dotted cave-key aliases; included the upstream
Skylands fixup. Upstream's 1.20 packer policy reuses these 1.21 translation files.

Witch Hut for their legacy structure IDs, and added dotted aliases for the
seven underground structure paths used by Explorer's Compass. Selected upstream
translation values are unchanged. GregTech Odyssey supplied the remaining
